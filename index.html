About

I am a PhD student in the Department of Digital Systems at the University of Piraeus, supervised by Professor Maria Halkidi. My research focuses on how federated recommender systems can adapt to diverse user histories while improving recommendation quality and examining performance disparities between user groups.

Activity-Conditioned Personalization for Federated Web Recommendation under Sparse User Histories

Georgios Karagkounis and Maria Halkidi

ACPFedRec combines metadata-based preference clustering, centroid smoothing, and activity-conditioned inference to personalize federated recommendation for users with different amounts of interaction history.
